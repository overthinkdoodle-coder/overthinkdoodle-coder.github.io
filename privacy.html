Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Overthink Doodle website (this site) and Overthink Doodle Publisher, a private publishing tool the Overthink Doodle creator uses to upload their own videos to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook through those platforms' official APIs.

Who runs this

Overthink Doodle is an independent creator. Contact: overthinkdoodle@gmail.com.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ads, and has no forms. Its host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Overthink Doodle Publisher

  • Who uses it: only the Overthink Doodle creator, for the Overthink Doodle accounts. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.
  • What it accesses: when the creator signs in with TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta, the tool receives the access tokens and basic account information (such as account ID, display name and avatar) that each platform returns, plus the creator's own videos, captions and their view, like and comment counts.
  • What it does with it: uploads and schedules the creator's own videos and shows their performance on a private dashboard. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Where it is stored: tokens and statistics stay on the creator's own computer. They are never published.
  • Data about other people: the tool does not collect information about viewers or other users beyond the public totals (views, likes, comments, followers) each platform reports for the creator's own posts.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in each platform's security settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), which deletes the tool's access. Stored tokens and data are deleted on request or when the tool stops being used.

Platform policies

Use of each platform is also governed by its own policies: the TikTok Privacy Policy, the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service, and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

The website and the tool are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their data.

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
